Goal: one page where staff upload the formatted bank file, filter a batch's lines by match status, reassign a line's TID (re-runs the 503 rules, audited), export the batch's matched lines, download the CSV and mark exported lines posted.

## Edge cases
Exported or posted lines are locked (409). Unknown TID gives 400, never a silent unmatched. A same-TID re-run is allowed and audited. A 0-exportable batch disables Export. A deleted tenant (FK null) is not exportable. A concurrent export and reassign are serialised by the advisory lock plus FOR UPDATE.
